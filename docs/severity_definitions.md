# Anomaly Severity Definitions

## LOW

Minor deviation with limited operational impact.

Examples:

- Small timing deviation
- Minor GPS deviation
- Low-confidence anomaly

Action:

Monitor and record.

---

## MEDIUM

Potential operational issue that requires review.

Examples:

- Repeated late check-ins
- Moderate route deviation
- Unusual patrol duration
- Repeated GPS quality problems

Action:

Operator review recommended.

---

## HIGH

Significant deviation that may indicate a security or
operational incident.

Examples:

- Missed checkpoint
- Significant route deviation
- Multiple missed check-ins
- Major timing anomaly
- Significant geofence violation

Action:

Immediate operator review and alert.

---

## CRITICAL

Severe anomaly requiring immediate investigation.

Examples:

- Guard disappears from expected route
- Multiple critical anomalies in a short period
- Major geofence violation
- Highly abnormal movement combined with missed
  check-ins
- Strongly suspicious behavioral pattern

Action:

Immediate escalation according to VigiloX operational
procedures.

---

## Severity Principles

Severity should consider:

1. Anomaly type
2. Anomaly confidence
3. Duration
4. Frequency
5. Historical behavior
6. Operational context
7. Combination of multiple anomalies
